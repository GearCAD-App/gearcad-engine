## Why GearCAD?

Most CAD tools show you what a robot looks like. GearCAD also shows you if it
works. It knows how gears, hinges and motors connect, so you can catch a
jammed gear train or a loose piece before you've built anything.

It's free and runs in your browser. No download, no account.

## What you can do

| Feature | What it means for you |
| :-- | :-- |
| 🧩 **Build with real parts** | 480+ official VEX IQ parts that snap onto holes, pins and shafts |
| ⚙️ **Check the mechanism** | See what turns, which way, how fast and with how much torque |
| 🕹️ **Simulate it** | Drive your robot with VEX IQ motors and real physics |
| 📏 **Stay legal** | Show the VIQRC and RECF Engage starting size limits |
| 🕓 **Never lose work** | Autosave and version history you can look back through in 3D |
| 📸 **Render it** | Studio-quality pictures of your robot, up to 4K |

## Get started

1. Open **[gearcadengine.web.app](https://gearcadengine.web.app)**
2. Click **New CAD** and pick a workspace
3. Click a part, move it over the grid and click again to place it

> [!TIP]
> Use **Chrome** or **Edge** for the full experience. They let GearCAD save
> straight to a `.gearcad` file (put it in Google Drive or OneDrive to open it
> on any computer) and they're needed for Render.

<details>
<summary><b>How versions work</b></summary>
<br>

GearCAD follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org). A bug fix bumps the
last number (`0.1.1`), a new feature the middle one (`0.2.0`). Early builds are
tagged `alpha`, `beta` or `rc`. Every update that goes live gets a
[release](https://github.com/GearCAD-App/gearcad-engine/releases) here with
what changed.

</details>
